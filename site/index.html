    Authority

    Autonomy without authority is just slop.

    Other tools remove the owner and hope for the best. Devys keeps you above the loop: agents act inside grants you can read, budgets you set and decisions you sign.

    Grants, not vibes

    Every employee's access is explicit: what it does alone, what asks first, what it can never touch.

    Signed decisions

    Approvals are signed on your iPhone or Mac and bound to the exact recipients, amounts and terms.

    Agents never hold your cards

    Card numbers and payment credentials stay in a separate vault. Agents hold handles, never the card.

    Receipts, not claims

    "Done" links to evidence: the deploy, the receipt, the reply. Uncertain outcomes get checked, never assumed.

    Manifesto

    Some people will hate this. Good.

    1. 01

      Headcount is a vanity metric.

      Leverage used to need permission: investors, hires. Agents don't. Judge a company by what it ships and how little of your attention it takes.

    2. 02

      This is not passive income.

      You design the system and make the hard calls. Agents do everything else. Anyone promising less is selling slop.

    3. 03

      Chat is not work.

      A message saying "done" isn't done. Commitments, records and receipts are.

    4. 04

      Not in the loop. Above it.

      You don't approve every step. You set the boundaries and decide the exceptions.

    5. 05

      If you're busy, the system is broken.

      Every interruption is a flaw in how the company is designed. Fix the system, not the symptom.

    6. 06

      Stop managing. Start designing.

      Your job isn't to supervise work. It's to build the machine that does it.

    7. 07

      Your company is yours.

      Your records, accounts, domains and exit. We will never take a cut of your revenue.

    8. 08

      Receipts, not demos.

      Every claim we make will link to evidence, starting with our own.
